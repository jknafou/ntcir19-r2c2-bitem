These animations are cut from the slides of a talk on this work, published at https://jknafou.github.io/epfl-r2c2/
They are made by the talk's own build, not by this repository: its paper-coloured ground, panels, greys and ink are laid on GitHub's light or dark ground, and every colour, the greys and ink included, is redrawn in a colour set of its own for each ground (Primer), except the illustrations of the question, the passages and the passage store, which keep the slide's own colours on its paper, framed as screens. The figures of the paper are built by docs/figures/build.sh.
